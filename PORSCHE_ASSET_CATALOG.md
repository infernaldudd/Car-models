## Restrictions

Do not import assets with NonCommercial, Editorial Only, or game-ripped provenance. 'Free download', 'royalty-free' or paid marketplace licenses do not automatically permit redistribution in a public GitHub repository. For CC BY/CC BY-SA, retain attribution and license details, identify changes, and comply with applicable ShareAlike terms. Separate vehicle-design/trademark issues remain.
