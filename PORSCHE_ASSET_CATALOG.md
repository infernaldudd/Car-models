# Porsche vehicle 3D model sourcing catalog

Updated 28 October 2026. Coverage: main road-car families, eight major 911 generations, racing families. These are **source leads**, not a guarantee of licenses, ownership, game-readiness, or legal vehicle branding.

**Legend:** CC BY = commercial use and redistribution with attribution if copyright-owner consent is valid; CC BY-SA = same, subject to ShareAlike obligations; NC = not commercial; paid/unknown = do NOT copy raw files into public repo. A third-party uploader's CC label cannot legitimize unauthorized game-extracted assets.

**Strong original-creator candidates:** Lionsharp 1975 930, Philipp Busse 1970s 911, _shobh19 lowpoly 993, Mikhail Hamanovich Taycan. These still require source/model review before shipping. For the reference user's 1981 911 SC, 1970s naturally aspirated 911 body is closer than the widebody 930 Turbo.

## Collection completion checklist

- 911 F 1964–73, G 1974–89 (SC and Turbo), 964, 993, 996, 997, 991, 992.
- 356, 550, 914, 924, 928, 944, 968, Boxster 986/987/981/718, Cayman 987/981/718, Carrera GT, 918 Spyder.
- Cayenne generations, Macan (petrol/EV), Panamera generations, Taycan.
- Racing/homologation: 904, 906, 908, 911 RSR, 917, 935, 956, 962, GT1, Carrera Cup, RS Spyder, 919, 963.
- Each raw model requires creator/source identity, model-specific license, attribution, provenance, compatible texture rights, export verification, QA and commercial right-to-use audit. **Do not clone marketplace models, game rips or NC models.**
