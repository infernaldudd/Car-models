### Design lock / anti-clone rules

1. Create design sheets independently from geometric principles and novel styling choices. Do not trace or import an actual Porsche or other OEM car mesh.
2. Build 1:1 only relative to **our own** approved metric design sheets; not 1:1 copies of third-party cars.
3. Reject silhouettes that too closely resemble an actual model, even after changing lights, crests or badges.
4. Build one high-detail master per concept, then derive optimized High/Medium/Low LODs; use genuine mechanical seams and clean junctions rather than artificial cracks.
5. Evaluate body matching, functional cabin, materials, engine/EV internals, no-gap inspection and performance before adding a GLB file.
6. Record all contributors and licenses before any final approval. Brand/product name clearance and legal review are still required.
