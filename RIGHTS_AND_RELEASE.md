# Original fictional car provenance and release gate

**Source:** Procedural Python generator `src/build_original_cars.py`; newly authored for the Helvetia/GundeliTheftAuto project. No third-party meshes, proprietary car files, brands, or photos used as model geometry. Colors and shapes are original authored design parameters.

**Five model IDs:** HV-S01, HV-S02, HV-S03, HV-S04, HV-S05. The IDs are internal identifiers, not claimed cleared commercial brand names.

**Commercial release:** The generator is self-authored original work. Nevertheless, rights to designs or names can overlap third-party claims, and AI-generated output may receive varying legal protection in different jurisdictions. No claim of zero legal risk or formal clearance is made. Human design-similarity and trademark review should precede sale.

**Legacy assets:** Removing Porsche models from the current Git tree does not wipe commit history, GitHub caches, forks, or clones. Historical artifacts must not be shipped.

**Production QA:** Five GLBs are functional prototypes and do not yet meet the original user requirement of photoreal, AAA-level fidelity. Exact status is recorded in `vehicles/GENERATION_REPORT.json`. Work remaining includes professional retopology and UV baking, surface refinement, textures, proper cabin access, body panels, engine and physics rig, performance LODs, Unreal review, and IP review.

**Workflow protection:** Neither the script nor the workflow should mark an asset `approved_for_release`. The build outputs are only `prototype_geometry_only`. Do not package prototypes into a commercial release by default.
